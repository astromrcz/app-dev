# 🎥 My Favorite Movies OAT!!!!

## 🐭 **Ratatouille (2007)**
*A delicious tale of dreams, flavor, and finding your place—even if you're a rat.*

- **Director:** Brad Bird  
- **Studio:** Pixar Animation Studios  
- **Genre:** Animated Comedy-Drama  
- **Summary:**  
  Remy, a rat with a passion for gourmet cooking, defies expectations by secretly teaming up with a young kitchen worker in a Parisian restaurant. Together, they prove that greatness can come from the most unexpected places.  
- **Themes:**  
  - Following your passion  
  - Challenging stereotypes  
  - The art of cooking and creativity

## 🧱 **Wall to Wall (2025)**
*A haunting urban thriller that turns everyday noise into psychological warfare.*

- **Director:** Kim Tae-joon  
- **Genre:** Korean Psychological Thriller  
- **Summary:**  
  Woo-seong finally secures his dream apartment in Seoul, but strange sounds and financial pressure begin to unravel his sanity. As tensions rise between neighbors and the housing market collapses, he must confront the walls closing in—literally and emotionally.  
- **Themes:**  
  - Urban isolation  
  - Mental health and financial stress  
  - The fragility of modern success

---

## 🛡️ **Iron Man (2008)**
*The origin of a legend—and the spark that ignited the Marvel Cinematic Universe.*

- **Director:** Jon Favreau  
- **Studio:** Marvel Studios  
- **Genre:** Action / Sci-Fi  
- **Summary:**  
  After surviving a kidnapping using a self-built armored suit, billionaire inventor Tony Stark reinvents himself as Iron Man. He chooses to use his genius and technology to fight injustice, setting the stage for a new era of heroes. Unironically the best character in MCU!
- **Themes:**  
  - Redemption and responsibility  
  - Technology as power  
  - Identity and transformation
